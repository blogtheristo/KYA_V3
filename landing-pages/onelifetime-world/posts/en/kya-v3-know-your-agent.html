KYA v3: Know Your Agent

Introduction

The evolution of autonomous AI agents demands a new paradigm for security, governance, and compliance. KYA v3 (Know Your Agent) provides a comprehensive framework for understanding the risks, rights, and responsibilities associated with AI agents in your ecosystem. This specification helps organizations protect themselves while ensuring responsible AI deployment.

What is open

The KYA v3 open specification defines standardized protocols for agent identification, authentication, authorization, and monitoring. It includes:

  • Open format definitions for agent metadata and capabilities
  • Standardized security patterns for agent communication
  • Transparent audit trails for agent decision-making
  • Framework for agent lifecycle management and revocation
By adopting open standards, organizations can ensure interoperability, prevent vendor lock-in, and maintain control over their AI infrastructure.

What is proprietary

Proprietary agent implementations may include:

  • Hidden decision-making algorithms
  • Black-box authentication mechanisms
  • Undocumented data access patterns
  • Vendor-specific integration protocols
These closed systems create unseen risks and dependencies that can compromise security and compliance. Without visibility into how agents operate, organizations cannot properly assess or mitigate potential threats.

Limitations of the open specification

While the open specification provides essential guardrails, it cannot guarantee absolute security. Implementation quality, human factors, and evolving threat landscapes require ongoing vigilance. The specification is a starting point, not a complete solution. Organizations must supplement it with robust security practices, regular audits, and continuous monitoring.

Why an open specification

An open specification enables:

  • Transparency in agent capabilities and behavior
  • Comparability across different agent implementations
  • Independent security assessments
  • Community-driven improvements and threat detection
  • Regulatory compliance through standardized reporting
Open standards foster trust, facilitate integration, and ensure that AI agents serve organizational goals without introducing hidden risks.

Conclusion

KYA v3 represents a critical step toward secure, transparent AI agent ecosystems. By understanding what is open versus proprietary, organizations can make informed decisions about their AI strategy. The specification provides a foundation for responsible agent governance, but successful deployment requires commitment to ongoing security practices and vigilance. As AI agents become increasingly integral to business operations, KYA v3 ensures that organizations remain in control of their technology stack and security posture.
